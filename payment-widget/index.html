Payment widget

redesign·skyeng·2024

Overview

CHALLENGE

The existing payment widget lacked the flexibility. We couldn't prioritize preferred payment methods, promote installment payments, or tailor the payment options to different business needs. Additionally, the widget lacked dedicated space for information about each payment method, making it harder for users to understand and compare their options. We needed to build a new, more flexible payment widget that could support new payment methods, provide greater control over their presentation, and improve the payment experience.

My role

  • Сonducted competitor research, analyzed the existing experience and designed the end-to-end customer journey from product selection to payment
  • Defined the structure of the payment widget and the logic for displaying different payment methods
  • Collaborated closely with the Product Owner, Product Managers, Engineering, Billing and Research teams throughout the project

Process

SEARCH AND CONCEPT

I started by conducting competitor research to identify familiar features and UX patterns, evaluate existing solutions, and explore opportunities to improve the payment experience. I then presented four design concepts to the team. Together, we selected two to validate through unmoderated user research.

UNMODERATED CONCEPT TESTING

Together with the research team, we explored how users perceived the design concepts, which layouts were easiest to understand, and what information mattered most to them. One key finding was that users could scan popular payment methods more easily in a horizontal layout than in a vertical list. These findings informed the final layout and helped us prioritize the most relevant payment methods.

DESIGNED NEW PAYMENT WIDGET

Based on the research findings, I designed a new payment widget. I refreshed the UI, highlighted the three preferred payment methods, and moved the remaining options into a dropdown menu. I also added details about each payment method, including payment schedules where available, to help users choose the option that best suited their needs.

ADDED SUPPORT FOR PROMO AND DISCOUNTS

The widget now can supports various installment plans and promo offers, making relevant payment options more visible to users. It also automatically applies users’ existing reward points to their payments.

BUILT A FLEXIBLE PAYMENT SYSTEM

The billing team can add new payment methods and update payment information without design support. The widget automatically adapts to different payment methods, while the business can reorder preferred options based on its needs.

A/B test

We launched an A/B test and found that the new widget underperformed the existing one on mobile version. After analyzing the data and testing several hypotheses, we suspected that the issue was related to the navigation in the payment system part. I simplified the mobile navigation, and we ran the experiment again. This time, the new widget outperformed. Following the successful test, we rolled out the widget to all traffic across Skyeng and Skysmart.
