Referral program for both referrers and referred users

redesign·skyeng·2025

Overview

Skyeng's original referral program rewarded both the referrer and the referred user with free lessons. As the school expanded its offering to include structured courses, the existing referral model became less suitable. Skyeng introduced a separate, cash-based referral program specifically for course students.

CHALLENGE

Both programs were no longer delivering the desired results, while maintaining them required significant resources from multiple teams. Our challenge was to unify both programs into a single solution that would work for all students, simplify reward management, increase user engagement, and drive more revenue through referrals.

Process

ANALYZED EXISTING REFERRAL PROGRAMS

To define our referral strategy, I analyzed existing referral programs. I mapped every user path, for both referrers and referred users which helped us understand the current issues with the referral program and identify opportunities for growth. Based on that, I suggested ideas to improve our referral program.

SEARCH

Conducted competitor research to understand which features and patterns were already familiar to users, which UX/UI solutions worked well or poorly, and what we could adopt.

KEY FINDINGS AND OPPORTUNITIES

After analyzing insights from UX research, competitor analysis, and user research conducted by our research team, we identified several key pain points and opportunities for improvement:

  • User engagement with the referral program declined, reducing their motivation to recommend the school to others.
  • Students were less inclined to recommend the school solely for personal gain. Their primary motivation was often the desire to help others.
  • Students lacked sufficient information about how the referral program worked and what benefits both the referrer and the referred user would receive.
  • We identified opportunities to strengthen existing entry points and introduce new when students would be more likely to share a referral link, such as after completing a homework assignment.

CREATED NEW REWARD SYSTEM

Instead of having two separate referral programs, we consolidated them into a single points-based system that worked for all students. This created a flexible, centrally managed rewards system that could work with different promotions, discounts, and products across the school. Students earned points directly in their accounts and could redeem them for other products offered by the school.

REDESIGNED THE REFERRAL EXPERIENCE

Redesigned the referral experience, introducing new UI patterns and elements. Users could track the status of their invitations, see how many points they had earned, and access the showcase to spend their points.

DESIGNED NEW LANDINGS PAGES

Created dedicated landing pages for both adult and kids’ products, giving invited users relevant information, an explanation of how the referral program works, and a clear path to apply

DESIGNED EMAILS AND STORIES

I designed a series of emails and in-app stories to communicate the new referral program, encourage participation, and increase user engagement.
