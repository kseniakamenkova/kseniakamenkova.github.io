Product catalog in the student's personal account

skyeng·2026

Overview

CHALLENGE

The product catalog was designed to solve several problems: • Increase the share of repeat purchases by making additional products and learning opportunities more visible • Bring Skyeng and Skysmart products together in one place, so students could explore available programs and learning formats and choose the ones that suit them • Let students quickly buy a product for another student • Lay the groundwork for a full shopping cart and the further development of self-service purchases

My role

  • Led design, competitor research, analyzed the existing experience, ran user research, and designed the end-to-end customer journey from product discovery to purchase
  • Developed a new visual language for the catalog
  • Collaborated closely with Design Lead, Product Manager and Engineering throughout the project

ResultsKey metrics after launch

+3%

Increased monthly recurring revenue by 3% by launching a self-service product catalog, with no increase in cost of sales and no drop in ATV

also:

  • The catalog was reused in the B2B funnel on the Ozon marketplace, increasing course purchases
  • The catalog became the basis for the catalog on the Skysmart homepage, letting prospective students explore and buy products

Process

SEARCH

Conducted competitor research to understand which features and patterns were already familiar to users, which UX/UI solutions worked well or poorly, and what we could adopt.

UNMODERATED USABILITY TESTING

Together with the research team, we explored how users discover products and what influences their purchasing decisions across different user journeys. The research revealed that pricing was one of the key decision-making factors, while users rarely explored filters beyond the basic options. These insights helped us refine the catalog experience and prioritize the features to include in the first iteration.

DESIGNED NEW CATALOG EXPERIENCE

I completely redesigned the existing experience, adding detailed product cards, filters, search, and favorites. We also automated product card creation, eliminating the need for manual input by the editorial team.

DEVELOPED A NEW VISUAL LANGUAGE

I created the visual language and a recognizable style for each product, making product information easier to scan and understand. Designed the hero images for each product, establishing a visual foundation that the Marketing team later built upon to expand and evolve product cards across other channels.

PURCHASE FOR OTHER STUDENTS

The catalog was expanded to include products for a wide range of learning goals and connected with the option to purchase products for other students, helping expand our student base while reducing reliance on the Sales team.

ADDED A NEW TOOL FOR MARKETING

The Marketing team can now use the catalog to promote campaigns and special offers.

EXPANDED BEYOND THE PERSONAL ACCOUNT

The catalog became the base for a catalog on the main Skysmart page, allowing prospective students to explore products and make purchase, and reusing the catalog in B2B funnel at Ozon marketplace which increased course purchases.
