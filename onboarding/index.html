Mobile app onboardings for adults and kids

redesign·skyeng·2026

Overview

Skyeng is the largest EdTech platform in Russia, helping both adults and children achieve their learning goals.

CHALLENGE

Thousands of students use the Skyeng app, but many of them drop off before finishing onboarding or stay on the free plan afterwards. Our goal was to increase onboarding completion and conversion to application, and to grow the number of students who find the right product for them and go on to make a purchase.

My role

  • Led end-to-end design, competitor research, analyzed existing experience and designed new flows and user scenarios
  • Developed a new visual language together with marketing team
  • Owned the product copy and content structure, working with UX-writers to ensure clarity and accuracy
  • Worked with a Project manager, Head of Product Design, mobile engineering and marketing

Results

As a result, we achieved better results than expected, and we rolled out the onboarding to 100% of our users.

Process

SEARCH

To set our direction, I conducted competitor research and analyzed the existing user experience. This helped us to understand which features and patterns were already familiar to users, which UX/UI solutions worked well or bad, and what we could adopt from our existing onboarding flows.

ANALYZED AND PREPARED SOLUTIONS

After several brainstorming sessions with the Product Manager and Design Lead, I developed two alternative user flows: one with a shorter onboarding process and a quick quiz, and another with more screens featuring stories about the school. We debated which approach would work better. The shorter flow allowed users to move through onboarding faster, while the longer one helped build trust in the school and increase the likelihood of purchasing a course. After weighing the pros and cons of both approaches, we decided to find a compromise by combining the best elements of each flow into one.

ADDED USER SEGMENTATION

To better understand users’ needs and recommend products that matched their educational goals, I added a quiz to segment users. The quiz was adapted from the web funnel, where it had already demonstrated strong results, and tailored to fit the app onboarding experience.

NEW DESIGN

To increase user engagement, I decided to completely redesign the onboarding experience by introducing mascots and creating new illustrations in collaboration with the Marketing team. These visual elements became part of the overall product identity and are now used across other communication channels.

PERSONALIZED OFFERS AND UPSELL

After completing the quiz, users received personalized offers based on their answers. This helped them find what they were looking for and allowed us to increase revenue through upselling. I also designed a personalized card for each product, highlighting its key features and the benefits of applying now. This encouraged users to submit applications sooner, increasing conversions while reducing the operational costs of acquiring customers through this channel.
